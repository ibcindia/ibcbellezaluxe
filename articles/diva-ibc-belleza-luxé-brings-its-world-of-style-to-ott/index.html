Diva: IBC Belleza Luxé Brings Its World of Style to OTT

Every great magazine has a point of view. For IBC Belleza Luxé, it has always been simple: beauty is not a look, it is a way of carrying yourself. Now that idea is moving from the page to the screen with Diva, a new OTT reality show built around style, self-belief and the stories behind the glamour.

More than a competition

Most reality shows ask who looks best. Diva asks a better question: who owns the room? Contestants are tested on fashion, grooming, poise, communication and creativity, but also on something harder to teach, which is presence. The show follows each participant through challenges, styling rounds and personal moments, so viewers see the person as well as the polish.

Where magazine meets screen

The same sections readers love on IBC Belleza Luxé shape the show’s world:

  • Fashion: styling challenges that reward individuality over trend-chasing.
  • Beauty: makeovers and skincare rituals that show confidence starts with care.
  • Lifestyle: real conversations about ambition, setbacks and reinvention.
  • Travel: striking locations that give every episode a distinct look.

Why OTT is the right home

Streaming lets Diva take its time. There is room for longer personal stories, behind-the-scenes moments and episodes viewers can watch whenever they choose. It also suits the audience IBC Belleza Luxé speaks to: readers who follow fashion on their phones and want entertainment that feels as considered as their wardrobe.

What viewers can expect

Expect bold styling, honest storytelling and a cast of contestants who bring very different backgrounds to the same stage. Diva celebrates the idea that elegance has many faces, and that anyone can learn to wear confidence well.

Stay with the story

Follow IBC Belleza Luxé for casting news, behind-the-scenes features and style guides inspired by the show. The magazine’s world is getting bigger, and Diva is its most glamorous step yet.
