The Runway Begins Here: Meet Omansh Gupta super kids

Omansh Gupta, 9, on his first runway walk, a talent hunt debut, and the confidence of beginning

Young Talent Spotlight | IBC Celebrity & Lifestyle

Standfirst: Nine years old and in Grade 3, Omansh Gupta is just starting to discover what it feels like to own a room. His first steps on a ramp came in a school classroom turned catwalk. Now he has a talent hunt behind him, and plenty of curiosity left.

Every fashion story has a first moment, a single walk that changes how a person sees themselves. For Omansh Gupta, it came at school.

The occasion was a Trash-to-Fashion competition, where everyday discards were reimagined as wearable ideas. Omansh stepped onto the ramp for the very first time. The lights, the audience and the quiet before the first step all arrived at once, and he walked anyway. What began as a school activity became his first real taste of style, self-expression and the thrill of the stage.

That spark led him to IBC Super Kids, a modelling talent hunt for children aged 3 to 12. There, Omansh stood among young hopefuls from different backgrounds and got his first experience of a proper talent show: the preparation, the nerves and the excitement of waiting in the wings. For a nine-year-old, it was a first look at the wider world of fashion and entertainment.

Glamour is not entirely new to him. Omansh comes from an entrepreneurial family that runs its own event business, so celebrations, stage setups and presentations have always been part of his surroundings. Growing up near the craft of creating occasions may explain how comfortable he looks when all eyes turn his way.

Still, his story is not about trophies or titles, and it does not need to be. At nine, the real work is discovering who you are. Omansh is learning how to carry himself, how to hold his nerve and how to enjoy the moment, which are lessons that outlast any single show.

The road ahead is long and open. Fashion, modelling and entertainment are all there if he wants them, and he has all the time in the world to decide.

For now, one thing is certain. A first walk, a new platform and a growing confidence are enough to start with.

Omansh Gupta’s story is only beginning.
